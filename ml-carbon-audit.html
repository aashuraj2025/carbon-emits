What to change first

    Method

    Energy (kWh) = (GPU TDP × count × draw% + host W) × hours × PUE / 1000
    Emissions (gCO₂e) = Energy (kWh) × grid intensity (gCO₂e/kWh)

    Preprocessing is assumed at 3% and validation at 5% of one training run. HPO energy = trials × trial length × one run. Replace these with measured values.

    Measure it for real

    pip install codecarbon

from codecarbon import EmissionsTracker
tracker = EmissionsTracker(project_name="my-run", measure_power_secs=15)
tracker.start()
try:
    train()          # your training loop
finally:
    kg = tracker.stop()
    print(f"{kg:.2f} kgCO2e")

    Alternatives: Eco2AI for per-run CSV reports, Carbontracker to predict emissions after the first epochs and stop early, Zeus for GPU power-limit tuning.

    Estimates use TDP-based modelling and approximate grid factors. Measured power is usually 60–90% of TDP. Use this to find the biggest levers, not to report official figures.
