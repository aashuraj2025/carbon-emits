How to cut it

    How it is calculated

    kWh/year = watts × load × hours/day × days/week × 52 / 1000 / charger efficiency
    Electricity kgCO₂e = kWh/year × grid (g/kWh) / 1000
    Manufacturing per year = manufacturing kgCO₂e / years kept

    To use real numbers, plug the laptop into a plug-in watt meter for a typical day, or check battery reports: powercfg /batteryreport on Windows, Activity Monitor's Energy tab on macOS, powertop on Linux.

    An estimate for awareness, not an official inventory. Manufacturing footprints vary by model; look up your manufacturer's product environmental report for an exact figure.
